| Sequence | Meaning                             | Equivalent To    |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `\d`     | Matches **any digit** (0–9)         | `[0-9]`          |
| `\D`     | Matches **non-digit** characters    | `[^0-9]`         |
| `\w`     | Matches **word characters**         | `[a-zA-Z0-9_]`   |
| `\W`     | Matches **non-word characters**     | `[^a-zA-Z0-9_]`  |
| `\s`     | Matches **whitespace** (space, tab) | `[ \t\n\r\f\v]`  |
| `\S`     | Matches **non-whitespace**          | `[^ \t\n\r\f\v]` |
